Research handoff, 2026-09-13. Owner: Alwin; reviewer: Melvin. Prepared as parallel milestone work; no production integration or model installation is claimed. The later real-photo crop policy exercise ran on iQOO; see corpus-study.md for its narrow result. GitHub had no PRs or issue comments at inspection; the existing remote speech branch contains speech research only.

## Recommendation and viewfinder behavior

Tier 0: optional grid/manual setup tips now; trial **edge clipping of a reliably detected single face** only after the gates below. Do not ship automatic exposure, beauty, background-tidiness, gaze or focus judgments on unvalidated heuristics. Tier 1: opted-in subject/background separation, lighting, focus, horizon and selected-product cues after each passes real-frame evaluation. No training recommendation until measured baseline errors identify a specific gap.

One small, dismissible prompt in the existing viewfinder, below script text and outside record controls; at most two lines. Offer only the highest-priority actionable fix. In recording, defer new prompts until an actual between-lines signal; if unavailable, show none. Never block recording or announce repeated unsolicited screen-reader updates. No scores, red failure badges, aesthetic grades, modal coaching, or changing camera settings automatically. Respect large text, contrast, reduced motion and the current safe-area layout. Turning coaching off clears queued prompts immediately.

Suggested priority: clipped selected subject → selected product obscured → confirmed focus issue → exposure → optional composition. Priority is a product hypothesis, not research evidence. Dismissal mutes that cue for the take. Loss of detection clears advice; it does not mean the creator left frame. No-face shots are normal. Initial candidate timing: stable issue ≥1.5 s, clear state ≥1 s, ≥15 s between distinct prompts, stale frame >500 ms discarded. Measure these values with creators before enabling by default.

## Short rubric

The education sources provide starting principles, **not numerical thresholds or universal aesthetic rules**. Gates below are proposed engineering hypotheses. Use visible crop coordinates after rotation/mirroring; never evaluate uncropped sensor coordinates.

| Area | Observable issue and useful wording | Intent and evidence limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Framing / headroom | Selected face intersects the visible frame for a sustained interval: “Give your face a little more room.” Head-box top is not hair/head extent. | Centered direct-to-camera and deliberate close crops are valid. Optional grid; never require thirds. Nikon [video composition](https://www.nikonusa.com/learn-and-explore/c/tips-and-techniques/video-composition-rules-establishing-medium-and-close-up-shots). |
| Look room | For an opted-in interview setup, visually inspect room toward the person's facing direction: “Leave a little room in front of you.” | Six face points do not establish reliable gaze. Disable automatic advice pending pose/intent validation. Same Nikon source. |
| Separation | Creator wants separation, and selected subject merges with surrounding structure: “Try a little space from the background.” | A person mask alone cannot establish visual confusion. Environmental storytelling and deep focus are valid. Nikon [composition guidelines](https://www.nikonusa.com/learn-and-explore/c/tips-and-techniques/5-easy-composition-guidelines). |
| Distracting background | In setup, creator reviews objects crossing the subject or competing with the product: “Check what’s behind you.” | Clutter, signs and workspaces can be meaningful. Never classify homes as bad; no automatic tidiness prompt. Same Nikon guidelines. |
| Backlight / exposure | Selected subject detail lost against bright surrounding areas: “Try facing the light.” For verified highlight clipping: “Try softer light.” | Silhouettes, low-key scenes and darker skin are not underexposure errors. Absolute face brightness is prohibited as the decision alone. Sony [video education](https://alphauniverse.com/academy/courses/video-101/) is the qualitative starting point; numerical tests need camera calibration. |
| Focus | Selected region persistently lacks recoverable detail after autofocus settles: “Tap your subject to focus.” | Intentional blur, rack focus, low-texture products and motion blur require suppression; do not promise tap-to-focus until integration supports it. Sony [product review tutorial](https://support.d-imaging.sony.co.jp/support/tutorial/dc/zv-1/en/14.php). |
| Horizon | Stable landscape shot, opted-in level aid: “Try leveling the phone.” | Handheld vlogs, pans, tabletop overhead shots and deliberate tilted angles suppress it; gravity is not necessarily a visual horizon. Nikon composition guidelines. |
| Distance / angle | Creator-selected shot cannot contain the intended subject: “Try moving back a little.” | Do not prescribe flattering face/body angles or infer real distance from face size; wide-angle perspective and intentional high/low shots vary. Nikon video composition. |
| Product visibility | Explicitly selected product leaves crop or becomes substantially occluded: “Bring the product fully into view.” | Hands/product-only mode suppresses face advice. Product emphasis can deliberately defocus the presenter; Sony product tutorial demonstrates this focus tradeoff. Generic object classes cannot cover arbitrary merchandise. |

Multiple people: no single-person composition advice without explicit subject selection; do not choose the largest/brightest face as the speaker. Handheld mode suppresses horizon/focus warnings during movement. A manual “intentional look” override suppresses composition/blur/lighting advice for the take.

## Proposed later production launch checks

These are recommendations for enabling production coaching, **not additional requirements invented for the current research milestone**. The current research deliverable is feasibility evidence and explicit limits. The [real-photo corpus study](corpus-study.md) and [scene-breadth additions](corpus-breadth.md) now supply nine licensed photographs, seven development viewport pairs and two reserved source-level held-outs. The breadth report also links the actual-camera negative/positive detector feasibility from #7. The pure [cue policy](cue-policy.ts) exports `runCoachingChecks()` for execution inside the iQOO app; it supplies no image detector and its results must not be reported as model precision.

Every automatic cue needs ≥100 positive and ≥100 negative **independent held-out clips**, plus intentionally unusual scenes. Split by creator, location, session and product before tuning; neighboring frames and paired takes stay in one split. These are proposed minimums, not proof of adequate statistical power. Report counts and confidence intervals, per-slice precision/recall and abstentions. Require ≥95% prompt precision, ≥80% recall on supported issues, ≤1 false prompt per 5 recording minutes, and zero prompts in explicitly suppressed modes. If any relevant skin-tone/lighting/shot-type slice lacks evidence, keep cue disabled rather than averaging it away.

| Candidate cue | Initial measurable trigger to evaluate | Specific release requirement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Edge clipping (Tier 0 trial) | One selected face, calibrated detection confidence ≥0.9; box within 2% of any crop edge for 1.5 s. | Annotated **actual visible clipping**, not bounding-box proximity, is ground truth. Reject candidate if close-but-valid faces trigger. Must pass rotated/mirrored front and rear crops and detection-loss recovery. |
| Product clipping (Tier 1) | User-selected product mask/box crosses crop; no inference when tracking confidence drops. | Positive/negative occlusion and entry/exit pairs, unseen products; never emit “show your face.” |
| Exposure (Tier 1) | Selected-region clipped-pixel proportion and contrast to background, calibrated per camera/HDR pipeline; no universal luminance cutoff. | Human detail-loss annotation across self-described skin tones, silhouettes and backlight. Detector miss is abstention. Until thresholds validated: manual tip only. |
| Focus (Tier 1) | Region sharpness relative to that region's settled baseline, camera AF state, no motion/intentional blur. | Distinguish defocus from motion, texture and low light. Validate before enabling; no fixed Laplacian threshold proposed. |
| Horizon (Tier 1) | Opted-in static level aid; gravity roll >5° for 1.5 s, clear below 3°. | Confirm upright pose and no deliberate tilt; disable for overhead shots or motion. |
| Separation / look room | No automatic launch trigger yet. | Require creator-labeled actionable confusion/intent and validated selected subject; remain manual guidance. |

Device budget proposal: sample ≤2 fps, never queue stale work, p95 end-to-end inference <100 ms, no recording interruption, no sustained frame-rate degradation >5% versus matched baseline over 10 minutes. These values need acceptance by #7 and are **unmeasured**. Disable coaching gracefully on model load failure, unsupported delegate or thermal pressure; recording remains available.
