## Actual camera-frame feasibility from #7

The capture lane's [device report](../f1/device-results.md) adds evidence beyond curated photographs. On the physical iQOO, its HTP detector returned **0.9921035** for the licensed positive yoga photo, with normalized box `[0.38127, 0.00627, 0.70557, 0.33057]`. The capture agent visually inspected that box as covering head/upper torso, **not the full body**. A saved actual-camera negative frame returned **0.00386168** and no detection at threshold 0.75. Private camera pixels are not published here. This is one positive and one actual-camera negative feasibility observation; it cannot estimate precision, recall, subgroup performance or advice quality.

Practical rubric consequence: do not use this detector box to claim complete body/headroom bounds. A low-confidence/no-detection frame must abstain rather than say “you are off frame.” Full pose needs correct ROI preprocessing and verified landmark confidence. The initial low landmark confidence is a processing gap, not a reason to train a new model. Recommend existing detector plus conservative rules first; training remains deferred until representative actual-frame evaluation exposes a specific baseline gap.

Current artifact scope is a sourced research proposal, a licensed paired-photo corpus with held-outs, scalar actual-camera model feasibility, and pure policy/device fixture checks. Remaining acceptance limits are representative actual **paired camera scenes** for supported cues and named human review/integration evidence; production launch sample-size targets are separate future gates.
