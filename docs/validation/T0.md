## A/V sync protocol

Record ten visible claps with an audible transient at the intended production camera settings.
For each sample, identify the video presentation timestamp of hand contact and the audio timestamp of the transient, using decoded media timestamps rather than host wall-clock time.
Record signed audio-minus-video offset, frame duration, and measurement uncertainty.
The spread across the ten offsets must be no greater than one frame at the tested frame rate.
Also report absolute offsets; a small spread alone does not establish acceptable lip-sync.
For variable frame rate, state which actual adjacent frame durations define the bound.

Record a two-minute take with identifiable synchronized events near its start and end.
Measure the change in A/V offset, disclose uncertainty, and attach the timestamp table.
Audio/video track-duration similarity alone is not a sync measurement.
Report drift and observed perceptual sync explicitly; do not invent an unapproved numerical drift acceptance threshold.
